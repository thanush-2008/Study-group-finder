 # 📚 Study Team Finder

Study Team Finder is a web-based prototype designed to help students connect with other learners and find study partners. It aims to make collaborative learning easier by bringing students together to share knowledge, discuss topics, and study as a team.

## 🌐 Live Demo

[Open Study Team Finder](https://studyteamfinder07.netlify.app/)

## ✨ Overview

- A prototype focused on helping students find study partners.
- Built to explore ways students can connect and learn collaboratively.
- Currently a small project and may evolve with future improvements.
